Questions

How do I begin?

Choose your path and pay securely with PayPal (Wise is available on request). You then receive an email invitation to access your program.

Which path is right for me?

The Guide gives you the foundations to apply on your own. The Program adds a structured 12-week plan in the Everfit app. The Private Experience is built entirely around you, with courses on your body, 1:1 calls and direct access to Kenji.

Is this medical advice?

No. INNÉ is a coaching and education experience. It does not replace a doctor, a dietitian or any other health professional. If you have a medical condition, seek medical advice before you start.
