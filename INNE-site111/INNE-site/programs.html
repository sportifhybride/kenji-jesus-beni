Which path is right for me?

The Foundation gives you the principles to apply on your own. The Reconnection adds a complete, structured program. The Private Experience is built entirely around you, with direct access to Kenji.

No. INNÉ is a coaching and education experience. It does not replace a doctor, a dietitian or any other health professional. If you have a medical condition, seek medical advice before you start.
